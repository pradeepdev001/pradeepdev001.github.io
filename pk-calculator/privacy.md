# PK Calculator privacy policy

<p class="meta">Effective 31 October 2026 · Android app <code>com.pktech.pkcalculator</code> · Made by Pradeep Dev · <a href="mailto:pradeepdev001@gmail.com">pradeepdev001@gmail.com</a></p>

> **PK Calculator collects no personal data.** There is no account, no ads, no
> analytics and no crash reporting. Your calculations stay on your phone.
>
> The only time it uses the internet is to fetch published exchange rates and
> gold and silver prices, and those requests carry nothing about you.

## What the app stores on your phone

Kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

| What                                                              | Why                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Your last 100 calculations, with their results and times          | The history screen                  |
| Your chosen currencies, and the last exchange rates fetched       | So the converter works offline      |
| The last gold and silver prices fetched, and any rate you type in | So the jewellery tools work offline |
| Theme, colour, vibration and key-sound settings                   | To remember your choices            |

You can clear the history in the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its
data, deletes everything above. Android backup is turned off for this app, so
none of it is copied to the cloud.

Dates you enter in the date and age tools are used for the calculation on screen
and are not saved.

## What leaves your phone

Nothing about you. Two screens fetch public price data:

- **Currency converter** — fetches current exchange rates from the
  ExchangeRate-API service (`open.er-api.com`).
- **Jewellery calculator** — fetches current gold and silver prices from
  Gold API (`api.gold-api.com`).

These requests ask only for the latest prices. They contain no information about
you, your calculations or your device. As with any request on the internet, those
services can see your IP address while answering it.

Copying a result puts it on your clipboard, and sharing opens Android's share
sheet — both only when you tap them, and you choose where it goes.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why                                         |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Internet   | Fetch exchange rates and gold/silver prices |
| Vibration  | Optional feedback when you press a key      |

The app does not ask for camera, location, contacts, storage, microphone or
notification permission.

## Children

PK Calculator is a general calculator, suitable for everyone. It collects no
data from anyone, including children.
