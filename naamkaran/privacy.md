# Naam Karan privacy policy

<p class="meta">Effective 1 November 2026 · Android app <code>com.pktech.naamkaran</code> · Made by Pradeep Dev · <a href="mailto:pradeepdev001@gmail.com">pradeepdev001@gmail.com</a></p>

> **Naam Karan does not ask for, or collect, any personal information.** There is
> no account, no ads and no analytics. Every name and meaning is built into the
> app, so searching and browsing happen entirely on your phone.
>
> The app uses two Google Firebase services: crash reporting, and push
> notifications for occasional announcements.

## What the app stores on your phone

Kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

| What                            | Why                      |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| The names you mark as favourite | Your favourites list     |
| Your language setting           | English or Hindi         |
| Theme and colour settings       | To remember your choices |

What you type in the search box is never saved or sent anywhere. Nothing about
your baby or your family is asked for or stored. Uninstalling the app, or
clearing its data, deletes everything above. Android backup is turned off for
this app.

## What leaves your phone

**Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).** If the app crashes, it sends the
technical details: where in the code it happened, your device model, Android
version, app version, and an installation identifier that Firebase creates.
Firebase also records when the app is opened, so that crash rates can be worked
out. None of this includes the names you searched for or saved. This is **on by
default** and can be turned off in **Settings › Privacy › Send crash reports**;
the change takes effect immediately and is remembered.

**Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).** When the app first starts, it asks
whether it may show notifications, and registers this installation with Google's
messaging service so that it can receive occasional general announcements, sent
to everyone alike. This gives the installation a token — an identifier held by
Google, never by us. If you decline notifications, nothing is shown. You can
change this at any time in your phone's settings for Naam Karan.

Both services are run by Google, which processes this data on the app's behalf
under the [Firebase privacy and security terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
Data is encrypted in transit. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone
else.

## Sharing, copying and listening

- **Share** opens Android's share sheet with the name, its meaning and a link to
  the app; you choose where it goes.
- **Copy** puts the name on your clipboard.
- **Listen** reads the name aloud using your phone's own text-to-speech.

All three happen only when you tap them.

## Permissions

| Permission                           | Why                                        |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Internet, network state              | Crash reports and notifications            |
| Notifications                        | Show announcements, only if you allow them |
| Wake lock, receive Firebase messages | Deliver notifications                      |

The app does not ask for camera, location, contacts, storage or microphone
permission.

## How long data is kept

- **Crash reports** are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
- **The messaging token** stops working when the app is uninstalled.
- **Everything on your phone** stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

To ask about, or ask for deletion of, crash reports from your installation, email
[pradeepdev001@gmail.com](mailto:pradeepdev001@gmail.com).

## Children

Naam Karan is made for parents and families choosing a name. It is not directed
at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If what the app collects ever changes, this page will be updated first and the
date at the top changed.
