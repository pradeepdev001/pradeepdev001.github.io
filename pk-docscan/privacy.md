# PK DocScan privacy policy

<p class="meta">Effective 31 October 2026 · Android app <code>com.pktech.pkdocscan</code> · Made by Pradeep Dev · <a href="mailto:pradeepdev001@gmail.com">pradeepdev001@gmail.com</a></p>

> **Your documents stay on your phone.** PK DocScan has no account, no ads, no
> analytics and no server. Scanning, text recognition and PDF tools all work on
> your device, and nothing is uploaded.
>
> Crash reporting is **off** unless you turn it on. Google's on-device
> text-recognition tools may send Google basic usage statistics, which never
> include your documents.

## What the app stores on your phone

Kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

| What                                                | Why                      |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Your scans, PDFs, edited photos and recognised text | Your documents           |
| QR codes and barcodes you make or scan              | Your history             |
| Scan history and settings                           | To remember your choices |

Temporary files are cleared every time the app starts. A document leaves the app
only when you share or export it yourself through Android's share sheet.
Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes everything above. Android
backup is turned off for this app.

## What leaves your phone

**Nothing you scan.** Document detection, text recognition (OCR) and barcode
reading are done on your device by Google's ML Kit and Google Play services. Your
images and their text are not sent to Google or anyone else for recognition.

**Usage statistics from Google's tools.** ML Kit and the Google Play services
document scanner may send Google limited information about how these features
perform — for example which feature ran, how long it took, and your device model
— so that Google can maintain them. This never includes your images or the text
in them, and the app cannot turn it off. It is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), only if you turn them on.** Off by
default. If you turn on crash reports in **Settings**, a crash sends the
technical details: where in the code it happened, your device model, Android
version, app version, and an installation identifier that Crashlytics creates.
While crash reports are on, Firebase also records when the app is opened, so
that crash rates can be worked out. Crash reports never include your documents. You can turn them off again at any
time. Firebase processes this data on the app's behalf under the
[Firebase privacy and security terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy),
encrypted in transit.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

**Links in QR codes** open only after you confirm, in your browser.

## Permissions

| Permission                        | Why                                                  |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Camera                            | Scanning documents and codes, only while you scan    |
| Photos and images                 | Opening pictures from your gallery to scan or edit   |
| Storage (Android 12 and earlier)  | The same, on older Android versions                  |
| Internet, network and Wi-Fi state | Optional crash reports, and Google's on-device tools |
| Vibration                         | Touch feedback                                       |
| Wake lock                         | Used by Google's libraries the app is built on       |

The app does not ask for location, contacts, microphone or notification
permission.

## How long data is kept

- **Crash reports**, if you turned them on, are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days,
  then deleted automatically.
- **Everything on your phone** stays until you delete it in the app, clear the
  app's data, or uninstall it.

To ask about, or ask for deletion of, crash reports from your installation, email
[pradeepdev001@gmail.com](mailto:pradeepdev001@gmail.com).

## Children

PK DocScan is a general document tool, not designed for children.
